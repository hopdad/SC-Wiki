The Meijer Supply Chain Wiki is now available. This knowledge base covers our end-to-end supply chain operations and the software systems that power them.

<!-- truncate -->

## What's Included

- **Supply Chain Knowledge** — Procurement, warehouse/DC operations, transportation, inventory management, and reverse logistics
- **Systems Documentation** — WMS, TMS, inventory systems, and integrations/EDI
- **Reference** — Glossary, acronyms, and team contacts

## Contributing

Editors can propose changes to any page using the "Propose Edit" button. Proposals go through a review workflow before being published.

See the [Setup Guide](/docs/reference/setup-guide) for details on the role and approval system.
