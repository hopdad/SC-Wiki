# Welcome to the Meijer SC Wiki

The Meijer Supply Chain Wiki is the central knowledge base for supply chain operations and the software systems that power them.

## What You'll Find Here

### Supply Chain Knowledge
Domain expertise covering the full lifecycle of Meijer's supply chain:
- **Procurement** — Sourcing, vendor management, and purchasing
- **Warehouse & DC Operations** — Receiving, putaway, picking, packing, and shipping
- **Transportation** — Routing, carrier management, fleet operations, and last-mile delivery
- **Inventory Management** — Replenishment, demand planning, and store-level inventory
- **Returns & Reverse Logistics** — Return processing and disposition

### Software Systems
Documentation for the technology suite that runs the supply chain:
- **WMS** — Warehouse Management System configuration, workflows, and troubleshooting
- **TMS** — Transportation Management System routing, carrier integrations
- **Inventory Systems** — Replenishment engine, ordering, and forecasting
- **Integrations & EDI** — EDI transaction sets, APIs, data flows, and trading partners
