# Fleet Operations

## Overview

Meijer operates a private fleet through **Meijer Logistics LLC** (USDOT #1459678) that handles a significant portion of distribution center-to-store deliveries. The fleet covers approximately **70 million miles per year** with a **99.8% on-time delivery rate** and has achieved no driver out-of-service incidents in consecutive years. Every store receives a fresh produce delivery every single day.

## Fleet Composition

| Vehicle Type | Description | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Day Cab Tractors** | No sleeper berth; for local/regional routes within HOS limits | DC-to-store routes within ~250 miles |
| **Sleeper Cab Tractors** | Equipped with sleeper berth for overnight routes | Longer routes, multi-day trips |
| **Dry Van Trailers** | Standard enclosed trailer, no temperature control | Grocery, GM, household products |
| **Refrigerated Trailers** | Temperature-controlled (single-temp) | Dairy, produce, frozen foods |
| **Multi-Temp Trailers** | Multiple temperature zones in one trailer | Mixed loads (frozen + refrigerated) |
| **Electric Semitrucks** | Freightliner eCascadia — all-electric, ~230 mile range | Local DC-to-store runs within 200-mile radius |

### Electric Fleet Initiative
Meijer was the **first retailer in North America** to run all-electric Freightliner eCascadia semitrucks in a cold-weather environment (December 2022). Key specs:
- **Range:** ~230 miles per charge
- **Battery:** ~440 kWh capacity
- **Recharge:** 80% in ~90 minutes
- **Emissions:** 40% reduction in well-to-wheel GHG vs. diesel
- Electric trucks operate from the Lansing DC on daily store runs
- Meijer has also reserved **Tesla Semis**

## Driver Management

### Driver Employment
- Meijer employs CDL Class A drivers as full-time team members
- Competitive pay, benefits, and home-time schedules designed for driver retention
- Most routes are designed for daily home-time (day cab operations)

### Scheduling
- Drivers assigned to routes based on seniority, familiarity, and equipment qualification
- Schedules built to maximize driving time within HOS regulations
- Relief and cover drivers available for absences and peak periods

### Hours of Service (HOS)
All drivers comply with FMCSA Hours of Service regulations:

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Driving Limit** | 11 hours after 10 consecutive hours off duty |
| **On-Duty Limit** | 14 hours after 10 consecutive hours off duty |
| **Rest Break** | 30-minute break required after 8 cumulative hours |
| **Weekly Limit** | 60 hours in 7 days or 70 hours in 8 days |
| **Restart** | 34 consecutive hours off duty resets weekly clock |

HOS is tracked via Electronic Logging Devices (ELDs) installed in all tractors.

## Vehicle Maintenance

### Preventive Maintenance Schedule
| Interval | Service | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | Pre-trip and post-trip inspections by driver | Tires, brakes, lights, fluids, reefer unit |
| **Every 15,000 miles** | PM-A (minor service) | Oil change, filter replacement, belt inspection |
| **Every 30,000 miles** | PM-B (intermediate service) | Brakes, suspension, electrical, HVAC |
| **Every 60,000 miles** | PM-C (major service) | Full drivetrain, transmission, differential |
| **Annual** | DOT annual inspection | Full Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards inspection |

### Maintenance Facilities
- Meijer operates on-site maintenance shops at major DC locations
- Third-party service providers used for road breakdowns and specialized repairs
- All maintenance tracked in fleet management system

## Telematics and GPS

All fleet vehicles are equipped with telematics that provide:
- **Real-time GPS location** — Visible to dispatch and store operations
- **Speed monitoring** — Alerts for speeding above governed limit (65 mph)
- **Idle time tracking** — Anti-idle policy enforcement
- **Hard braking and acceleration events** — Safety behavior monitoring
- **Reefer temperature monitoring** — Continuous logging for refrigerated/frozen trailers
- **Fuel consumption data** — MPG tracking per vehicle and driver
- **Geofence alerts** — Automated check-in/check-out at DCs and stores

## Safety Program

- **DOT compliance** — All vehicles meet FMCSA and DOT regulatory requirements
- **Driver training** — Ongoing defensive driving, Smith System, and seasonal driving programs
- **Incident reporting** — All accidents and near-misses documented and investigated
- **CSA monitoring** — Carrier Safety Administration scores tracked; proactive remediation
- **Drug and alcohol testing** — Per FMCSA requirements (pre-employment, random, post-accident)

## Fleet vs. Third-Party Decision

| Factor | Private Fleet | Third-Party Carrier |
|---|---|---|
| **Control** | High — direct management of drivers and equipment | Lower — dependent on carrier priorities |
| **Service quality** | Consistent — trained Meijer employees | Variable — depends on carrier |
| **Cost (high volume)** | Generally lower per mile at scale | Higher per mile but no fixed overhead |
| **Flexibility** | Limited by fleet size | Can scale up/down quickly |
| **Backhaul revenue** | Can generate revenue hauling for others | Not applicable |
| **Capital investment** | Significant — trucks, trailers, maintenance | None — pay per load |

Meijer uses the private fleet for core, predictable lanes and third-party carriers for overflow, seasonal peaks, and non-core routes.
